Privacy Policy

Spintelligence · effective 28 October 2026 · applies to the web app, the Android app and the Windows app

Short version: Spintelligence does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no server of ours. Everything the app knows lives on your own device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your arsenal: the list of Beyblade models and custom combos you add. It is saved in the app's local storage on the device (the browser's local storage for the web app, the app's private storage on Android, %APPDATA%\BeybladeCounter on Windows). It never leaves the device unless you export it yourself.
  • An offline copy of the app (web app only): the pages, the database of published part stats and the product photos are cached by your browser so the app works without a connection.

You can delete all of it at any time: use Wipe arsenal inside the app, clear the site's data in your browser, clear the app's data or uninstall it on Android, or delete the folder above on Windows.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal information or device permission. The Android app declares only the standard network permission, which it uses solely to open the links described below when you tap them.
  • It does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising or any third-party SDK.
  • It does not send your arsenal, your searches or anything else to us or to anyone. All assessments are computed on the device.
  • It does not use cookies.

Network connections

The Android app and the Windows app work entirely offline. The web app is a static site served by GitHub Pages; when you load it, GitHub's servers receive the ordinary technical data any web server sees (your IP address, browser type and the pages requested), which GitHub handles under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive or look at that data. Links inside the app to the Beyblade Wiki or to GitHub open those sites in your browser, under their own privacy policies.

Export and import

Export JSON writes your arsenal to a file you choose; Import JSON reads such a file back. The file contains only model names and part combinations. What you do with that file is up to you.

Children

Spintelligence is a reference tool for players of all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either. It is not directed at children under 13 and contains no social features, purchases or advertising.

Third-party content and trademarks

Spintelligence is an unofficial fan-made tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Takara Tomy, Hasbro or the Beyblade franchise. Beyblade and Beyblade X are trademarks of their respective owners. Part and product stats are the values published by Takara Tomy and Hasbro as transcribed by the Beyblade Wiki (CC BY-SA); product names and photos are used only to identify the parts.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated first and the effective date above will change. The current version is always at chromechris.github.io/beyblade-counter/privacy.html.
